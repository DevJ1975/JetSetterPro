# Packing for a short business trip

For a two-to-four-day business trip, aim for carry-on only to skip baggage claim and reduce loss
risk.

## Capsule wardrobe
Pack one suit or blazer in a neutral color, two or three shirts/blouses, one pair of versatile
shoes, and layers you can mix and match. Roll casual items and use packing cubes; keep the jacket on
top or in a garment fold to limit wrinkles.

## The essentials kit
Laptop and charger, phone charger and a power bank (in carry-on), travel adapter for the
destination's plug type, noise-canceling headphones, and any printed materials you'll need before
your bag is accessible.

## Weather and season
Check the destination's forecast and add one weather-appropriate layer — a packable rain shell for
wet climates, or a warm mid-layer for cold ones. For warm destinations, prioritize breathable
fabrics.

## Documents
Passport or ID, any required visa/ETA approval, boarding passes, hotel confirmation, and a backup of
key documents stored securely offline.
