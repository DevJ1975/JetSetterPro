# US entry: ESTA and the Visa Waiver Program

Citizens of Visa Waiver Program (VWP) countries can visit the United States for tourism or business
for up to 90 days without a visa, but must hold an approved ESTA (Electronic System for Travel
Authorization) before boarding.

## Applying for ESTA
Apply online at least 72 hours before travel. An approval is typically valid for two years or until
your passport expires, whichever comes first, and covers multiple visits. You need a machine-readable
e-passport.

## Things that disqualify visa-free travel
Travelers who have visited certain countries since March 2011, or who hold dual nationality with
specific countries, may be ineligible for ESTA and must apply for a B1/B2 visa instead.

## On arrival
Have your return ticket, address of stay, and ESTA approval ready. Customs and Border Protection
makes the final admission decision at the port of entry.
