# Schengen Area entry requirements

The Schengen Area lets travelers move between most European countries without internal border
checks. For short stays, many non-EU visitors (including US, UK, Canadian, Australian, and Japanese
passport holders) may enter visa-free.

## The 90/180 rule
Visa-free visitors may stay up to 90 days within any rolling 180-day period across the entire
Schengen Area combined — not per country. Overstaying can lead to fines and entry bans.

## Passport validity
Your passport must be valid for at least three months beyond your planned departure from the
Schengen Area, and should have been issued within the previous 10 years. Carry at least two blank
pages.

## ETIAS (from 2025 onward)
Visa-exempt travelers will need an approved ETIAS travel authorization before arrival. It is an
online pre-screening tied to your passport, valid for multiple trips over several years. Apply
before booking non-refundable travel.

## At the border
Be ready to show proof of onward/return travel, accommodation, and sufficient funds. Travel medical
insurance is strongly recommended and may be requested.
