# Airline loyalty and elite status

## How status works
Airlines award elite tiers (e.g., Silver, Gold, Platinum) based on miles or segments flown, or on
revenue spent, within a calendar or rolling year. Higher tiers unlock priority boarding, free
checked bags, lounge access, and upgrade priority.

## Earning efficiently
Concentrate flying within one airline or its alliance (Star Alliance, Oneworld, SkyTeam) so credits
accumulate toward a single status. Co-branded credit cards often grant a head start, lounge passes,
or a free checked bag that pays for itself on a few trips.

## Status matching and challenges
When switching airlines, ask about status matching: many carriers will temporarily grant comparable
status, sometimes with a short "challenge" to requalify by flying a set amount.

## Using status well
Set your frequent-flyer number in every booking so segments credit automatically. Two segments short
of the next tier near year-end can justify a mileage run or a strategically routed trip.
