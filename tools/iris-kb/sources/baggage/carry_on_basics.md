# Carry-on baggage basics

## Liquids: the 3-1-1 rule
Liquids, gels, and aerosols in carry-on must be in containers of 100 ml (3.4 oz) or less, together
in a single transparent, resealable 1-quart/1-liter bag — one bag per passenger. Larger liquids go
in checked baggage. Medications, baby formula, and breast milk are exempt but should be declared at
screening.

## Size and weight
Carry-on allowances vary by airline, but a common limit is roughly 56 x 36 x 23 cm including handles
and wheels. Many low-cost carriers charge for anything beyond a small under-seat personal item, so
check your fare before you fly.

## Power banks and lithium batteries
Spare lithium batteries and power banks must travel in carry-on, never checked. Power banks above
100 Wh may require airline approval; above 160 Wh are generally prohibited.

## Prohibited in carry-on
Sharp objects, most tools over 7 inches, and flammable items must be checked or left behind. Sharp or
dangerous goods rules are set by aviation security, not the airline.
